3ds Media Manager

Browse, sort and star your 3DS and DS screenshots on any device

Everything runs in this browser tab. Your screenshots are never uploaded anywhere.

Plug in your SD card and open it

Select the root of the SD card (the top-level folder, not a subfolder). 3ds Media Manager automatically looks for luma/screenshots, 3ds/Comet/ds_screenshots, _nds/nds-bootstrap/screenshots.tar, and DCIM inside it and loads whichever of those it finds — nothing else on the card is touched or read.

Just have the raw screenshots.tar on its own?

Fan-made companion app for the managing screenshots on the Nintendo 3DS — not affiliated with Nintendo. Sound off, always private.
